Timestamp authority material

This is not a licence. It records what four files of this repository are,
where they came from, and why they are here. They were made by two public
timestamp authorities, Free TSA (www.freetsa.org) and Sectigo Limited, not
by Tzun, Inc. Tzun claims no copyright in them and grants no licence to
them: the BSD 3-Clause Clear License and CC0-1.0 that cover the rest of
this repository do not apply to them. Whatever rights exist in them are
their issuers'.

An authority publishes its root certificate, and returns a signed
response to whoever requests a timestamp, so that anyone can verify the
timestamp. The files are here, their content unchanged, for that purpose
alone: the tests verify each response against its authority's root
(internal/rfc3161/rfc3161_test.go, internal/corpus/realtsa_test.go). The
build does not use them, and no release binary contains them.

Both responses are over one SHA-256 digest, the record digest of corpus
case anchored-with-newer:

  9739ebd76cc45223ccc88920802c9032208f3c09f67fc129a41a769109dead6a

  internal/rfc3161/testdata/freetsa.tsr
    The RFC 3161 response the service at https://freetsa.org/tsr returned
    to a request over the digest above, kept as received. Its token is
    dated 2026-10-01T19:40:02Z and carries the Free TSA signing
    certificate and the root above.

Sectigo Limited

  internal/rfc3161/testdata/sectigo-root-r46.pem
    The Sectigo Public Time Stamping Root R46 certificate, in PEM. Its DER
    is byte for byte the file Sectigo publishes at
    http://crt.sectigo.com/SectigoPublicTimeStampingRootR46.crt.
    SHA-256 of the certificate (DER):
      4941b001b8a97e961b7817c9d9e960ec4b056bfc915a8c1aabf6ef6b3ac046a5

  internal/rfc3161/testdata/sectigo.tsr
    The RFC 3161 response the service at http://timestamp.sectigo.com
    returned to a request over the digest above, kept as received. Its
    token is dated 2026-10-01T19:40:03Z and carries the Sectigo Public
    Time Stamping Signer R37 certificate, the Sectigo Public Time Stamping
    CA R41 certificate, and a certificate for Root R46 issued by USERTrust
    RSA Certification Authority, a root Sectigo operates.

To obtain these files from their source rather than from this repository,
download the two roots from the addresses above, and request new
responses from the two services, for example with
`openssl ts -query -digest <digest> -sha256 -cert`.
